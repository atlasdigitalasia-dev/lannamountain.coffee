The challenge

Great coffee doesn't
sell itself to cafes.

Every roaster we've worked with has the same problem: exceptional product, a handful of wholesale accounts from word of mouth, and no systematic way to grow the pipeline. The same is true for equipment suppliers trying to reach roasteries, and importers pitching roasters on new origins.

Cold outreach in coffee has a bad reputation because it's usually done badly — generic emails, wrong contacts, no follow-up. Done well, with the right targeting, the right message, and the right timing, it's still the most effective way to land new wholesale accounts.

We know the coffee industry. We know how cafe owners think, what roasters care about, and what makes an importer pitch credible. That industry knowledge is what separates our outreach from a generic email blast.

  • Targeted prospect lists built around your ideal buyer profile
  • Personalised sequences — not mail merges
  • Email, WhatsApp, and LinkedIn combined for ASEAN and Western markets
  • Follow-up cadences that respect the relationship
  • CRM setup so nothing falls through the cracks

How we reach them

The right channel for
the right market.

Coffee outreach isn't one-size-fits-all. The channels that work in Melbourne are different to those that work in Bangkok or London. We know the difference.

📧

Email sequences

Carefully written, personalised email sequences — not mail merges. 3–5 touch points with genuine value at each stage. Works best for UK, AU, and NZ markets where email is the primary B2B channel.

💬

WhatsApp outreach

Essential for ASEAN markets. WhatsApp is how cafe owners and roasters communicate in Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, and Indonesia. We build outreach sequences that respect local communication norms.

💼

LinkedIn for trade buyers

For equipment suppliers and importers targeting procurement managers, F&B directors, and roastery owners. LinkedIn connection + message sequences that build relationships before pitching.

🗂️

CRM & pipeline management

We set up and manage a simple CRM so every prospect, response, and follow-up is tracked. You get a clean pipeline view and monthly report — no leads ever falling through the cracks.

How it works

First outreach in
two weeks.

01

Ideal buyer profile

We define your ideal prospect — cafe size, location, style, spend level, existing supplier relationship — and build a targeted list. Quality over quantity every time.

02

Sequence & messaging

We write all outreach copy — email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn. Every message is reviewed and approved by you before anything goes out. No surprises, no off-brand messaging.

03

Launch & manage

We run the outreach, handle initial responses, book calls and sample requests, and pass warm leads to you when they're ready to discuss an account.

04

Report & refine

Monthly pipeline report with open rates, response rates, and conversion data. We refine the targeting and messaging each month based on what's working.
