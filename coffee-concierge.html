What Coffee Concierge does

More than a chatbot.
A coffee expert.

Coffee Concierge knows your products, your origin stories, your brewing guides and your wholesale terms, so every answer sounds like it came from someone who really knows your coffee.

☕

Personalised recommendations

Asks about brewing method, flavour preferences, roast preference and experience level, then recommends the right coffee from your range. Like having a knowledgeable barista on your website 24/7.

📦

Synced with your store

Connects to your Shopify or WooCommerce catalogue and syncs automatically, so it only recommends what's actually in stock, at the right price. Other platforms can sync from a Google Sheet.

🏪

Wholesale enquiry handling

When a cafe owner or restaurant buyer asks about trade pricing, Coffee Concierge switches mode, captures their details and qualifies the enquiry, then routes it to you to follow up.

❓

FAQ & education

Answers questions about origin, process, grind size, brew ratios, subscriptions and shipping, cutting down support emails and giving buyers confidence before they purchase.
