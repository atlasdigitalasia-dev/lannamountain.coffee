The challenge

Coffee SEO requires
industry knowledge.

Generic SEO agencies treat coffee like any other e-commerce vertical. They optimise for high-volume consumer terms and ignore the B2B buyer journey entirely — missing the wholesale accounts, trade buyers, and import/export relationships that drive the most revenue.

Coffee has unique keyword categories that general agencies don't recognise: origin terms, varietal searches, process method queries, certification-specific searches, and trade buyer intent terms that convert at much higher value than retail traffic.

We've built SEO strategies for roasters in New Zealand, Australia, and the UK. We know which terms convert wholesale accounts, which content builds credibility with trade buyers, and how to structure a coffee site so both search engines and AI tools understand what you do.

  • Origin & varietal keyword strategies (Chiang Rai, Natural, Honey, Washed)
  • Wholesale buyer intent keywords most agencies completely miss
  • Certification content that ranks and builds trade credibility
  • B2B content strategy for importers, exporters and suppliers
  • Runs alongside GEO for combined AI + traditional search coverage
