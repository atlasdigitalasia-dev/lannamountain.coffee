01

Why now, not more patches

The case from our call

When we spoke, the sticking point was clear: wholesale cafés order by phone and email, and you're left chasing payment by hand. You've also got a full range of coffee machines that almost nobody browsing the site knows you sell. September's audit found the same gap from the search side — the site wins on story, and loses the moment intent turns commercial.

Wholesale searches, page 1
0/3
Zero first-page presence for wholesale-intent searches checked — one lost directly to another West Coast roaster.
Local searches, page 1
7/10
Three of them at #1 — Kawatiri already wins on brand, story and certification searches.
SEO score
66/100
Needs work — concentrated in missing commercial pages, not in the fundamentals.
Machines listed online
0
You sell coffee machines. The site doesn't show them yet.

The argument in one line

Wholesale runs on your phone. It should run on your site.

Right now your wholesale cafés order by calling or emailing you, and you invoice and chase payment after the fact. A discount code in your existing shop lets them order and pay as they go — no separate portal to log into, no manual invoicing on your end. It's the single biggest change in this proposal, and the wholesale page, the machines pages and the SEO fixes all support it: more of the right visitors finding a site that's actually built to sell to them.

Two signature pieces

The change that matters most, twice over

Two things matter most here — the change that gets you out of the phone-and-email loop, and the page that gives your wholesale customers somewhere to land.

★

Wholesale, on autopilot

Once it's live, a new wholesale account is just a code. Existing cafés keep ordering the way they always have — through your normal shop — they just don't have to call, email, or wait on an invoice, and neither do you. It turns wholesale from something you manage by hand into something the site handles for you.

◎

The Wholesale page closes the search gap

Kōkako and IncaFé — the two comparable organic/Fair-Trade roasters checked in September's audit — both have a dedicated wholesale page. Kawatiri doesn't, and it's the direct, provable reason you lose all three wholesale-intent searches checked, including one to a genuine West Coast competitor. This page is built specifically to close that gap.
