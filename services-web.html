Why it matters

Coffee websites aren't
like other websites.

A roaster's website needs to do something no generic e-commerce template is built for: sell retail bags to consumers, convert wholesale inquiries from cafes, tell an origin story credibly enough to justify a premium price, and rank for the specific search terms buyers in your market actually use.

An importer's site needs to signal trust, traceability, and professional credibility to roasters who are staking their own brand reputation on your supply chain. A supplier's site needs to generate B2B leads, not retail clicks.

We've built around ten specialty roaster websites across New Zealand, Australia, and the UK. We know what works — and what generic agencies get wrong every time.

  • Wholesale inquiry flows that qualify leads before they reach you
  • Origin storytelling structured for both buyers and search engines
  • GEO-ready from day one — built to be cited by AI tools
  • Mobile-first, fast-loading, and technically clean

What we build

Three types of coffee
website. One specialist.

Different businesses in the coffee supply chain need fundamentally different websites. Here's how we approach each one.

🫘

Roaster websites

The most complex coffee website to get right. Needs to serve retail consumers, wholesale buyers, and subscription customers — each with different journeys and different content needs.

  • E-commerce with subscription logic
  • Roast date & freshness UX
  • Wholesale inquiry portal
  • Origin & sourcing storytelling
  • Blend & single-origin product pages
  • GEO-optimised from launch
Most requested
✈️

Importer & exporter sites

Credibility is everything. Roasters staking their brand on your supply chain need to see traceability, certifications, origin depth, and professional trust signals before they ever make contact.

  • Origin & traceability content
  • Certification & compliance pages
  • Farm relationship storytelling
  • Trade buyer inquiry flows
  • Multilingual (EN, Thai, Bahasa)
  • Schema for AI citability
⚙️

Supplier & equipment sites

B2B lead generation for equipment, packaging, and wholesale suppliers. Needs to reach roasteries and cafe groups systematically — not just look good in a portfolio.

  • Product catalogue & specs
  • B2B lead capture flows
  • Case study & install gallery
  • Trade pricing inquiry forms
  • Integration with outreach CRM
  • Distributor & reseller pages
What's included

Every build includes
the full stack.

No hidden extras. Every website we build comes with everything needed to rank, convert, and be found by AI tools from day one.

📱

Mobile-first design

Over 60% of coffee research happens on mobile. Every build is designed mobile-first, not adapted from desktop — faster load times, better UX, higher conversion on the devices your buyers actually use.

⚡

Performance optimised

Slow sites lose buyers before they read a word. We build clean, lightweight code with optimised images, efficient scripts, and hosting-agnostic performance that scores well on Core Web Vitals.

🤖

GEO-ready from launch

Every site we build is structured with AI citability in mind from day one — correct schema, entity signals, and content architecture that gives AI tools what they need to recommend your business.

🔍

SEO foundations built in

Technical SEO isn't an add-on — it's part of every build. Correct heading hierarchy, meta structure, canonical tags, sitemap, robots.txt, and coffee-specific keyword foundations included.

🔒

SSL, security & hosting advice

Every site ships with SSL, security best practices, and honest advice on hosting options for your market and budget — whether you're in Australia, the UK, or Southeast Asia.

📊

Analytics & tracking setup

Google Analytics 4, Search Console, and conversion tracking configured from launch — so you know from day one which pages are working, where buyers are dropping off, and what to fix.

How it works

Live in 4–6 weeks.

A focused, no-fluff process. No endless discovery phases. No committee sign-offs. Just a clean build that gets your business online properly.

01

Discovery call & brief

One focused call to understand your business, buyers, markets, and goals. We produce a written brief you approve before any design begins — no surprises downstream.

02

Design & content

We design and write simultaneously — because content shapes design in coffee websites, not the other way around. One round of revisions included. We don't design in a vacuum.
